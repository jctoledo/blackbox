What this replay shows

  • The two completed laps had uninterrupted 40 ms receiver-epoch intervals in the exported GPS log: 25 Hz during those laps. This does not establish physical timing accuracy.
  • Stored lap times come from the track export. Replay boundaries were reconstructed from the exported timing line. Positions use host receipt times and are interpolated between retained samples; this is an approximate replay.
  • Speed is GPS receiver speed in km/h. The chart aligns elapsed time, so its points do not necessarily represent the same location on the route. It is not the dashboard’s matched-position reference delta.
  • Only relative metre coordinates, elapsed seconds and speeds are included. The route shape remains real; absolute latitude/longitude, street maps, original timestamps and raw recording files are omitted.
