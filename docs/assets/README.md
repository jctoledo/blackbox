# Website media

`road-hero.mp4` and `road-poster.jpg` are adapted from **Slow Motion Video of Driving on City Road** by **Andrew Pakho**:

- Source: https://www.pexels.com/video/slow-motion-video-of-driving-on-city-road-5103753/
- Source download: https://videos.pexels.com/video-files/5103753/5103753-hd_1920_1080_25fps.mp4
- License: https://www.pexels.com/license/ (checked 2026-11-20)
- Pexels help confirms free commercial use: https://help.pexels.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042295214

The footage remains under the Pexels license, not the repository's MIT software license. It is used as decorative stock footage and does not depict a Blackbox test or imply endorsement by the contributor.

Adaptation performed on lat with FFmpeg: source seconds 5–15, crop 1600×700 at 0,0, resize to 1280×560, slow by 1.4×, 24 fps, desaturate, slightly darken, fade the first/last second, remove audio, H.264 CRF 29, fast-start MP4. Poster is a frame at 3 seconds of the adapted clip.

The page uses a dark overlay, a pause control, offscreen/hidden-tab pause, reduced-motion still fallback, and data-saving suppression. The video source is attached only when playback is permitted. Replace both clip and poster with appropriately licensed owner footage later, retaining the same interface and documenting provenance.
