# Cockpit and website acceptance — November 2026

This captures the maintainer's requirements from the implementation chat.

- Show speed with three digits, leading padding zeros at 20% opacity. Apply consistent leading integer zero treatment to displayed times; fade time punctuation too. Preserve exported numeric values and fractional precision. Stable bundled numeric fonts must prevent horizontal glyph shifts and work without internet on the device.
- Review and simplify cockpit hierarchy, copy, alignments and type with multiple independent agents. Rebuild a tasteful, prominent G-meter from actual telemetry; validate lateral orientation against supplied recent recordings. Use occupant-load direction, explicit per-axis scale, combined horizontal magnitude, freshness gates and unchanged raw CSV signs. Include compact speed in landscape instruments and retain valid reference progress.
- Research and provide a small set of useful rolling ranges plus experimental 0–60 mph / 0–100 km/h and quarter-mile timing. Retain evidence, units, provenance, rejection states and explicit launch convention; avoid unsupported physical-accuracy claims. See RUNS.md and research/PERFORMANCE_RUNS.md.
- Persist synthetic circuit, rolling and standing drives. Expose record/save/read-only replay through the website and embedded dashboard, using the same stored-data path. Keep synthetic examples labeled and private real recordings out of source/public assets. Replay must not mutate live timing, tracks, statistics or recordings, and must handle gaps, missing data, storage errors and asynchronous switching.
- Refresh the complete public site's look and feel to match the revised device UI, including guides, existing real-drive page, screenshot library and homepage playback entry. Keep current firmware installer and compatibility safeguards.
- For new circuit or stage/segment capture, require the car stationary for five seconds, collect multiple distinct accepted GPS fixes, and average the start position. Show a simple countdown and cancellation. Movement or interrupted GPS resets it. Anchor the start-line center to the average; derive direction from the subsequent route. Ordinary recordings and existing timing flows keep their behavior.
- Finish and address independent UI/code review, run required checks on lat, merge reviewed changes to main and deploy/verify the website. This is explicitly authorized by the maintainer. Do not flash hardware or claim desktop checks establish physical race readiness.
